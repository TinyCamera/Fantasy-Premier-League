How to play it

  • Round 1 picks itself. Haaland at +126, then Bruno +94 and Gabriel +86. If all three are gone, you are into the plateau and should just take the best forward.
  • Rounds 2–4: get two of the top five forwards. The 4th forward scored 172, the 6th scored 128, and the worst starting forward in the league scores 95. That drop is steeper than anywhere else on the board.
  • Never reach for a midfielder. Replacement is 126pt — the highest of any position. There is no midfield cliff, so every early round spent there is value burned.
  • Defence is the value pocket. 30 defenders sit in one broad tier. Let them come to you in the middle rounds instead of paying up early.
  • Goalkeeper: Raya or last. He is +35; the next keeper is +26 and it is flat from there to +3. There is no middle ground worth a pick.
  • The projection already rescues the injured. Adding 2024-25 lifted Isak from 268th to inside the top 120 and put Palmer back in the top 60. What it cannot fix is a player whose role changed, so trust last season over the blend when someone has clearly just won a starting job.
  • EST players are guesses, not projections. Anyone marked EST has never played in this league. Only two of them are worth a pick at all — treat the rest as waiver-wire names you are aware of, not draft targets.
